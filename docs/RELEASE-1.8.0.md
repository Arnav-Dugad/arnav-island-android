# Arnav Island for Android 1.8.0: all of it, in glass

If you have 1.7.0, this version installs itself. Update your PC's island to 0.26 too: shuffle and repeat, each app's volume, the live levels, Wake, pausing and the camera and microphone need it.

## A new look, after Apple's
- **Liquid glass throughout,** calmer and clearer: Apple's system colours, rounder continuous corners, and grouped lists with an icon on its own colour for each row, as in iOS Settings.
- **Large titles** scroll away into a glass navigation bar, with what's under it blurred.
- **Sheets follow the back gesture:** as you swipe back, a sheet shrinks and sinks with your finger, and springs back if you change your mind.
- **Numbers keep still:** digits are all the same width, so times, percentages and speeds no longer jitter as they change.
- **What just happened** drops from the top like an iOS notification. Swipe it up to let it go.
- **The island at the top of the app and the weather on the glass are gone.**

## The Remote
- **Live levels:** the music's own level bars dance under the cover (on the same Wi-Fi or a direct path).
- **Shuffle and repeat,** for players that report them, and **Up next** for music your PC's island plays from your library.
- **Love a song:** the heart keeps it in your Loved list.
- **Recently played:** what you've heard on your PCs, with covers. Tap one to play it on your PC, find it on this phone, love it or remove it.
- **Sound:** where your PC's sound plays from, a tap away, and **each app's own volume**, with its mute and its level now.
- **The volume buttons turn your PC's sound** while the Remote shows (Settings › On this phone).

## The Island tab, a dashboard
- **Overview:** your PC with its model, Windows version, uptime and connection, and four live rings (processor, graphics, memory, disk).
- **Performance:** the processor and graphics with graphs that glide from one reading to the next, each core, memory and disk as bars, the network both ways, and this session's peaks, averages and data moved.
- **Then:** battery, sound (each app too), controls, focus, the command bar, power, alerts, pages, island settings, and **About** (processor, graphics, memory, disk, Windows, its network adapters).
- **Every section folds away,** and once the title has scrolled off, a row of their names stays at the top to jump to any of them.

## Alerts
**Settings › Alerts & live updates › Alerts:** a notification when your PC's processor or graphics card has been busy for a while, its memory or disk runs short, its battery runs low, or it has finished charging. Each is set by its own level.

## Send and the Shelf
- **Pause a transfer** and carry on later (with island 0.26).
- **One send at a time to each PC:** the rest wait in **Up next.** Touch and hold one to move it.
- **Recent** shows the pictures of what came, and you can swipe one away.
- **The Shelf:** pull down to look again (a drop of glass stretches out of the top), **Select** to take several, **Take all**, and touch and hold one to peek at it.

## Devices and Settings
- **Settings** have their own sheet: grouped pages and a search across all of them.
- **What's left to set up** shows on Devices, each with a Fix button.
- **Each PC's details:** its own name, colour and icon (on this phone), when it was last seen, and its **Connection**: the path, its round trip over time, the relays, IPv6 and the encryption, with **Test again**.
- **Wake your PC** from the phone, on the same Wi-Fi, when it sleeps or is off (its adapter must allow Wake-on-LAN).

## New ways to use your PC
- **Present:** big Next and Back buttons, the volume buttons flip slides, a laser pointer you aim with the phone, and a timer that buzzes at five minutes, one minute and time up.
- **Air mouse:** point the phone and the pointer follows (Trackpad › Air mouse).
- **This phone as your PC's camera or microphone:** the camera opens in a window on your PC (capture it with OBS's virtual camera for calls). The microphone plays into VB-CABLE there, so any app can use it.

## On your home screen
- **Three new widgets:** the song's lyrics line by line, both batteries side by side, and the transfer under way. Each comes in a small and a large size.
- **A live wallpaper:** the app's light in the colours of what plays on your PC (Settings › On this phone › Live wallpaper).
- **Live updates** (Android 16): transfers, and your PC's song counting down, in the status bar's chip and on the lock screen.

## Checks
- **Unit tests:** 19 passing, among them the wake packet and the new answers read from the PC.
- **Against the island's engine over the network:** protocol, the whole island, screens both ways, photos and pages, all passing.

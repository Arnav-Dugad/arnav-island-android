# Arnav Island for Android 1.9.0: on its way

If you have 1.8.0, this version installs itself. Update your PC's island to 0.27 too: live activities, transfers that carry on, temperatures, the clipboard history, slides, the speed test and the compressed microphone need it.

## What's on its way, on your PC
**Your Swiggy, Zomato or Blinkit order, a ride, a timer: on your PC's island, with its tracking line.** When something on this phone shows how far it has got (a delivery, a ride, Maps, a timer, a download, Android 16 live updates), the island on your PC shows it as a live activity: the app's icon and the time left at rest, and opened, the tracking line in the app's colours with the rider moving along it. A green tick when it's delivered. Turn it on at **Settings › Live activities on your PC** (it uses the notification access the app already has). Notifications you hide on the lock screen are never sent.

## Gemini, for your PC
**Ask Gemini on this phone to control your PC:** "pause the music on my PC", "turn my PC's volume down", "lock my PC", "send this to my PC", "what's my PC's battery?". On Android 16 the app offers its actions to Gemini directly; elsewhere, the app's shortcuts and a voice action work with "Hey Google, open Arnav Island…". **Settings › Use with Gemini** lists what you can say.

## The presenter's laser works again
The laser pointer in **Present** follows the phone again; it had stopped as soon as you pressed it. **Air mouse** can now be calibrated (hold the phone as you like, tap Calibrate), and flicks move between slides. With island 0.27, the presenter shows the **slide, the next one and your speaker notes**, and jumps to any slide.

## Transfers that carry on
**When the connection drops part way, nothing is lost.** A transfer carries on from where it stopped as soon as your PC is back, both ways, up to five times. What arrived is kept for a day.

## More of your PC
- **Temperatures:** the processor's and graphics card's, on the Island tab next to the connection and in About, with an alert when they run hot (Settings › Alerts).
- **The last hour:** the Island tab's Performance shows the hour's processor, graphics and memory, kept even when the app was closed.
- **Your Island tab, your order:** Edit to reorder its sections and pin the ones you want on top.
- **Clipboard history:** what you copied on this phone and on your PC, in one list. Copy any of it here or there, pin it, or forget it.
- **Speed test:** your PC's connection both ways, in its Connection page.

## Send and the Shelf
- **Send to all your PCs** at once, from Send and from the share sheet.
- **The share sheet, as AirDrop's:** each PC as an avatar in its own colour, a ring as it goes, a tick when it's there.
- **Recent:** search it, filter it by kind, and new arrivals fly in.
- **The Shelf:** sort by newest, name, size or kind, and open a folder to see what's in it.

## Also
- **Two new widgets:** your PC's controls, and each app's volume.
- **The camera as your PC's camera** is lighter on the network, and with island 0.27 on Windows 11 it can be a real Windows camera any app picks. The **microphone** now comes over compressed (Opus, else AAC), smooth through the relay too.
- **Pairing, redone:** your PCs on this Wi-Fi appear as avatars on the radar; the QR code and typed code below, as a list.
- **A short tour** of what's new, once, after this update.
- **Polish everywhere:** tighter spacing, steadier animations, and fixes to sheets, banners and the light theme.

## Checks
- **Unit tests:** 31, 0 failures (5 skipped without a PC), among them the live activity encoder, the wake packet and the island's new answers.
- **Against the island 0.27's engine over the network:** protocol, the whole island, screens both ways, photos and pages, transfers that carry on, all passing.
- **On an Android 16 emulator, against the test PC:** temperatures, the clipboard history both ways, the speed test, the mixer's app icons, Wake and last seen, Send to all, Recent's search and filters, pairing and the light theme.
- **Not yet tried:** live activities from real delivery apps (made-up ones used), Gemini on a real phone, and the PC side of the Windows camera.
- **Not in this version:** ending an app on your PC, browsing your PC's files and taking its screenshot from the phone. The buttons for them stay hidden until the island supports them.

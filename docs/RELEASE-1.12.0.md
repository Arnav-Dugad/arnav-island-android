# Arnav Island for Android 1.12.0: every notification, in full

If you have 1.11.0, this version installs itself. Update your PC's island to 0.29 too: everything below shows there.

## Your notifications, in full on your PC
- **Chats arrive as chats.** Your PC gets the conversation, not just the newest line: who said what in a group, and your own replies.
- **Faces and photos.** The sender's picture and a photo a message carries go along. Turn it off in Settings › **Photos in notifications**.
- **Suggested replies.** The replies Android suggests (and an app's own quick answers) show on your PC as one-click buttons.
- **Three actions** instead of two, such as Reply, Mark as read and Archive.
- **Clear it from the PC.** Closing a notification's card on your PC clears it here too. You can turn that off on the PC.
- **Codes.** Your PC finds a verification code in a notification and offers to copy it.

## Deliveries and rides, made for India
- **More apps** count as deliveries: Myntra, Meesho, AJIO, Nykaa, Tata 1mg, PharmEasy, magicpin, and redBus for your bus. Any app whose ongoing notification reads like a delivery or a ride counts too ("out for delivery", "delivery partner", "your ride").
- **What the app's own card says** goes to your PC with it, so the island can read the OTP to give, the cash to pay and the cab's number, even from apps that draw their own notification.
- **Rides are told apart from deliveries**, so on the PC you can choose to see only your deliveries.

## Checks
- Unit tests, including the new notification layout and the ride and words in live activities: all passing.
- Against the PC's own engine (island 0.29) over the network: the notification in full (chat, faces, photo, suggested replies, three actions), the PC's confirmation that an action went, and the PC clearing a notification here.
